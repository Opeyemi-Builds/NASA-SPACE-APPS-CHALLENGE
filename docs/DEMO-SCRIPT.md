# 3-Minute Demo Script

Target: judges see the full story — green baseline, drift, pattern rule, guidance — with zero manual fixes.

## 0:00–0:20 — Landing
- Open the public link. Point at the vitals panel: Day 12 already shows sleep Alert, heart Watch.
- One line: "It learns each astronaut's normal, spots drift early, and tells them what to do next — even with a 20-minute Mars delay."
- Click **Enter App**.

## 0:20–0:50 — Day 7: all green
- Drag the demo day slider to **Day 7**. Everything OK.
- Say: "Days 1–7 set the personal baseline. Mood, vision and reactions are judged against *you*, not a poster."

## 0:50–1:30 — Days 10–11: the slide
- Drag to **Day 10**: sleep + Head/brain zone turn Watch. Tap the zone — show the trend chart with the shaded normal band.
- Drag to **Day 11**: sleep hits Alert (3rd night under 6 hours). Read the "what to do now" + "who to tell" lines.

## 1:30–2:20 — Day 12: the pattern rule
- Drag to **Day 12**: mood + heart + exercise join Watch → overall **Alert**. Point at the "signs add up" pill.
- Open **Check-in**, enter one reading live (e.g. heart rate 105) to show instant Watch.
- Hit the **URGENT** button once so they see the red sheet is reachable from everywhere.

## 2:20–3:00 — Proof + Learn
- Open **Trends**: scroll two charts, point at hazard tags.
- Open **Tests**: "Every handover test case runs live against the engine — 15/15."
- Close: "Works offline — service worker. All data simulated, banner on every screen."
