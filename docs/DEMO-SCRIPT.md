# 3-Minute Demo Script

Target: judges see the full story — green baseline, drift, pattern rule, guidance — with zero manual fixes.

Flow is numbered 1–5 in the bottom nav: Today → Check-in → Trends → Learn → Checks.

## 0:00–0:20 — Landing
- Open the public link. Hero shows the live solar system.
- One line: "It learns each astronaut's normal, spots drift early, and tells them what to do next — even with a 20-minute Mars delay."
- Click **Enter App — Day 12**.

## 0:20–0:50 — 1 · Today (Day 7: all green)
- Step to **Day 7** with Prev/Next. Everything OK.
- Say: "Days 1–7 set the personal baseline. Mood, vision and reactions are judged against *you*, not a poster."
- Tap the 3D body — a region opens status → action → who to tell.

## 0:50–1:30 — 1 · Today (Days 10–11: the slide)
- Step to **Day 10**: sleep + Head/brain zone turn Watch. Attention list shows max 3 items.
- Step to **Day 11**: sleep hits Alert (3rd night under 6 hours). Read the "who to tell" line.

## 1:30–2:20 — 1–2 · Day 12: the pattern rule + live check-in
- Step to **Day 12**: mood + heart + exercise join Watch → overall **Alert**. Point at the "Signs add up" pill.
- Open **2 · Check-in**, enter one reading live (e.g. heart rate 105) to show instant Watch.
- Hit the **URGENT** button once so they see the red sheet is reachable from everywhere.

## 2:20–3:00 — 3–5 · Proof
- Open **3 · Trends**: troubled signals first, shaded normal band.
- Open **4 · Learn**: one line per signal, hazards behind a toggle.
- Open **5 · Checks**: story checks up top, full engine table behind a toggle.
- Close: "Works offline — service worker. All data simulated, banner on every screen."
